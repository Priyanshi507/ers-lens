Did the FIA's Miami fix change super-clipping?

From the Miami Grand Prix, a car may harvest up to 350 kW while flat out, not 250 kW. Real races cannot show what that changed, because each circuit raced under one rule. So every circuit is solved twice for the lap-time-optimal energy strategy, once under each cap, with everything else equal.

What this can't tell you

These are lap-time optima for a simplified model car on one lap with mid-race fuel: no traffic, tyres, slipstream or Overtake mode. They show what the rule makes worth doing, not what real teams did. Fourteen of the eighteen circuits use an assumed recharge limit. Whether real laps moved the same way has not been tested yet.
