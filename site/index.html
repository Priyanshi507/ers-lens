What did clipping cost each car in 2026?

In 2026 an F1 car's electric motor fades out near the end of long straights, a drop the paddock calls clipping. Public telemetry can't show the battery, but it can show the speed lost. This site measures that loss, lap by lap.

    What this can't tell you

    It can't tell you how much charge a car has left. Cars with different engines and drag can produce the same speed trace while using very different amounts of battery energy, up to 2.5 MJ on one straight. So this site measures what clipping costs in lap time, which public data does support, and not the battery itself.
