How far to trust this

Every lap here is a lap-time optimum of a simplified car, proven within 0.01 s of the best lap on the model. Tested against real 2026 laps, the model gets directions right and sizes wrong: it clips where real cars clip at 13 of 16 races, and where real qualifying and race laps differ it moves the same way at 6 of 7, but its speed loss is about twice the real one and it does not rank circuits as real cars do. Read the change between two rules, not the size under one. The 2027 speed curve is not published, so four possible curves are offered.
