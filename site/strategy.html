What is the fastest way to spend the battery?

For each circuit, dynamic programming finds the lap-time-optimal electric power at every 5 m of track under the 2026 energy rules, with the battery ending the lap no emptier than it started. Every optimum is replayed in the lap simulator and kept only if the two agree within 0.05 s.

Seven predictions, three cars

The predictions were written down before any code. The car could not be calibrated to a single drag and engine-power value, because the four pole laps prefer different points on a ridge of equally good fits. So every prediction was tested on three cars, and a result counts as robust only if all three agree. Hover over a cell for the measured values.

What this can't tell you

These are model cars, not real ones. The calibration failed its own acceptance test, so modelled lap times carry about ±0.7 s per lap of uncertainty between circuits, and the pole laps are assumed to be close to energy-optimal. Overtake mode, slipstream and traffic are not modelled. Real batteries are not observable, so comparisons with real laps are exploratory.
